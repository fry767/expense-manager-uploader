Personal Android app updates

A convenient place for the latest APK.

Expense Manager APK Uploader sends signed Android builds of the Expense Manager app to the owner's Google Drive. It helps the owner install updates on their phones without connecting each phone to a computer.

How it works

  1. The owner authorizes the uploader with their Google account.
  2. GitHub Actions builds and signs the Android app.
  3. The uploader creates an Expense Manager APKs folder in Drive and keeps expense-manager-latest.apk up to date.
  4. The owner downloads the APK on their phone and confirms installation.

Limited Google Drive access

The uploader requests Google's drive.file permission to access the files and folders it creates. It uses that access to upload the APK, update the existing APK, and obtain its private download link. This website does not sign you in or access your Drive.

This is a utility for the owner's personal use. APKs stay in the owner's Drive; the uploader does not make them public or change their sharing permissions.
